# CBDR Privacy Policy

**CBDR does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** The app has
no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server — it
works entirely offline on your device. The microphone and speech recognition
are used only when you choose to speak a contact report, and recognition runs
entirely on-device; audio never leaves your phone. The camera is used only to
scan QR codes shown on another participant's screen, and group-test scores
are exchanged solely through those QR codes and stored only on the devices
involved. Training settings and in-progress test sessions are saved locally
on your device and nowhere else. The optional feedback buttons simply open
your own mail app with a pre-addressed draft — nothing is ever sent without
you pressing send yourself.

## Support

CBDR is an offline training tool and has no server component. For questions,
